# Food Delivery Platform Data Modeling Assignment

## Business Scenario

A food delivery company provides the following operational data:

| OrderId | Customer | Restaurant | Items | Prices | Driver |
|----------|----------|------------|--------|--------|--------|
| 1001 | John | Pizza House | Pizza, Coke | 15, 3 | Mike |

The data represents customer orders delivered from restaurants to customers by delivery drivers.

---

## Assignment Tasks

### Task 1: Create a Database Model

Design a relational database that accurately models the business domain.

Provide:

1. Conceptual model
2. Logical model
3. Physical model

### Task 2: Identify master and transaction entities

### Task 3: Normalize the source data (3NF)

### Additional Task 1 (for extra credits): Provide physical data model as code
