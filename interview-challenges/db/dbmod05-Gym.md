# Gym Domain Model

Gym has members and trainers. Members create subscriptions. Trainers lead classes which could be subscribed by members. Member can also book a trainer for a private training session.

Task: Design a conceptual domain model.

## New Requirements

* How you make sure trainer is not overallocated? E.g. trainer's activities do not overlap.
* Individual equipment reservations are supported.
* Multi-branch support.
* Shared training sessions with capacity.
* Gym has capacity. Digital check-ins are supported.
* Members could register for individual class session.
* Classes schedule exceptions. E.g. this week's Monday spinning class at 6PM will take place on Tuesday at 3PM.
