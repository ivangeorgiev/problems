## P32 - A problem of prime factors

What is the largest number whose prime factors (excluding unity) add up to exactly 1DO?

Just to be sure that we're all on the same system, the prime
factors of 12 are 2,2, and 3 (since 2x2x3=12). Their sum is 7.

Similarly, the prime factors of 90 are 2,3,3, and 5, and their sum is 13.
